I am an R&D Staff Engineer at Children's National Hospital in Washington, D.C., where I work on surgical navigation, robot-guided imaging, and AI/ML and LLM systems for healthcare.

I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from Emory University, where I was also a research assistant and a teaching assistant, and a B.E. in Computer Engineering from Devi Ahilya University.

My research interests include: Evaluation, Post-Training, Recursive Self Improvement, Human-AI Interaction, Reinforcement Learning, Computer Vision, Privacy & Security.
